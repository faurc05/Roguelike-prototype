# ⚔️ PyRogue — Dungeon Roguelike

PyRogue is a classic-inspired, procedurally generated dungeon roguelike focused on exploration, turn-based combat, loot, and character progression.

Every dungeon floor is generated as you play, creating different rooms, corridors, enemies, and item locations. Explore carefully, fight monsters, collect equipment, gain experience, and descend deeper into the dungeon.

## 🎮 Controls

### Movement

* **Arrow Keys** — Move
* **H J K L** — Move
* **Y U B N** — Diagonal movement

### Combat

* **Walk into an enemy** — Attack

Combat is turn-based. Every movement or attack gives enemies an opportunity to act, so positioning and timing matter.

### Items & Inventory

* **G** — Pick up an item
* **I** — Open inventory
* **Item letter** — Use or equip an item

Items can include:

* Health Potions
* Weapons
* Armor
* Scrolls

Weapons increase your attack, armor improves your defense, and consumables can help you survive difficult encounters.

### Dungeon

* **>** — Descend to the next floor
* **Q** — Quit the game

## 👹 Enemies

The prototype currently features several enemy types with different stats and difficulty:

* **Rat** — Weak and fast
* **Goblin** — Basic dungeon enemy
* **Orc** — Stronger melee enemy
* **Skeleton** — Durable undead enemy
* **Troll** — Powerful high-health enemy
* **Dragon** — Extremely dangerous late-game enemy

Enemies become stronger as you descend deeper.

## 📈 Character Progression

Defeating enemies grants XP. When you gain enough experience, you level up and become stronger.

Leveling up increases:

* Maximum HP
* Attack
* Defense

Your goal is to become powerful enough to survive the increasingly dangerous dungeon floors.

## 🗺️ Procedural Dungeons

Dungeon layouts are generated procedurally, meaning each new floor can have a different arrangement of rooms and corridors.

Explore the dungeon to find:

* Enemies
* Items
* Hidden areas
* The stairs to the next floor

The deeper you go, the more dangerous the dungeon becomes.

## ✨ Visual Features

PyRogue includes several visual effects designed to make the prototype feel more alive:

* Glowing stairs
* Glowing items
* Animated player effects
* Enemy health bars
* Floating damage numbers
* Hit flashes
* Dark unexplored areas
* Dungeon tile variations
* HP and XP bars
* Inventory interface

## 🔊 Audio

The game uses procedurally generated sound effects, so no external audio files are required.

Sounds are generated for:

* Attacks
* Enemy hits
* Item pickups
* Equipment
* Level ups
* Death
* Descending floors
* Ambient dungeon audio

## 💀 Death

PyRogue is designed around the classic roguelike concept of dangerous runs.

If your HP reaches zero, your character dies and the current run ends.

How deep can you get before the dungeon takes you?

## 🚧 Prototype Status

PyRogue is currently a **playable prototype**.

The main gameplay loop is implemented, but the project is still actively being developed. Future versions may introduce more enemies, weapons, abilities, bosses, dungeon types, progression systems, visual effects, and other gameplay features.

## 🎯 Current Gameplay Loop

**Explore → Fight → Loot → Level Up → Descend → Survive**

The deeper you travel, the stronger the enemies become and the more important your equipment and decisions become.
